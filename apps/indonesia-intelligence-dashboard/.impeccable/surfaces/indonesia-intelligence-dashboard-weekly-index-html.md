# Weekly Indonesia Intelligence

Mode: Operate. Audience: an Indonesian professional making career and family contingency decisions. Task: scan one week, inspect evidence, and choose what to monitor or prepare. Constraints: preserve the dashboard system, source every story, expose uncertainty, and never present news sentiment as a personal-safety guarantee.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A weekly decision room that turns news volume into inspectable preparation signals, refusing a sensational danger meter.

OWN-WORLD: Existing cool white and navy surfaces, blue controls, Geist text, compact charts, restrained green, amber, and red status semantics.

STORY: Read the weekly status, see why it moved, inspect topic sentiment and highlighted stories, then review family triggers and destination options.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Persistent dashboard navigation above a status band; left side holds the three-level verdict and confidence, right side holds a seven-day sentiment line and evidence counts.

FORM: Focused extension of the established analytical dashboard; no new visual world or seed round.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
