Changes for AiFn: use original german.data (not the numeric variant).
Select original attributes 2,5,8,16,13 and class. Keep numeric values;
rename to duration,amount,installmentRate,existingCredits,age. Map source
class 1=good / 2=bad to risk 0=good / 1=bad. Add sourceRow as a local 1-based
line ordinal; it is NOT a source customer ID. No dates or outcomes added.

Units: UCI explicitly describes duration in months and age in years.
Attribute5 is described as credit amount but its official units entry is
null and german.doc does not specify its currency. Other attributes mention
DM, but that is not proof of Attribute5 units. AiFn therefore labels amount
as original/source credit-amount units, NOT rupiah and without conversion.
Installment rate retains integer source values 1-4; documentation describes
percentage of disposable income but supplies no further thresholds here.
Do not invent a category-to-percentage mapping.

risk means historical good/bad creditworthiness in this dataset. It is NOT
a verified default within 90 days. No application timestamps exist in this
extract. The random split is NOT out-of-time validation. No representation
of Indonesia or contemporary borrowers is claimed.
